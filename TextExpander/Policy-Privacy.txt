TextExpander Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

TextExpander is a Chrome extension designed to help users save time by automatically expanding user-defined shortcuts into full phrases.

1. Information We Collect

TextExpander does not collect or transmit personal information to external servers.

The extension processes text entered into webpage input fields, textareas, and contenteditable areas only to detect user-defined shortcuts and replace them with their corresponding phrases.

2. Shortcut Data

TextExpander stores user-created shortcuts and their corresponding phrases locally in the user's browser using Chrome's local storage.

For example:

brb → Be right back

This shortcut data is stored locally on the user's device and is not uploaded to or stored on external servers.

3. Website Content

TextExpander requires access to webpage text input areas so that it can detect shortcuts and perform text expansion.

Text entered by the user is processed locally within the browser for the sole purpose of providing the text expansion functionality.

TextExpander does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit the user's typed content to external servers.

4. Data Sharing

TextExpander does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

The extension does not send shortcut data or typed text to external servers.

5. Data Security

Shortcut data is stored locally using Chrome Storage. TextExpander does not maintain a remote database containing users' shortcut information.

6. Analytics and Tracking

TextExpander does not use third-party analytics services, advertising trackers, or tracking technologies to monitor users.

7. User Control

Users can create, view, edit, search, and delete their saved shortcuts through the TextExpander management page.

Users can also remove the TextExpander extension from Chrome at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

TextExpander does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the extension or applicable requirements.

Any updated version of this Privacy Policy will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or TextExpander, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
